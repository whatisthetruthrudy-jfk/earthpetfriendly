Simple buying guides

Start with fit

Measure your pet, feeding space, carrier space, and habitat before shopping. Check weight limits as well as dimensions.

Read the materials and care label

Look for clear material descriptions and realistic cleaning instructions. Choose items you can keep clean and maintain.

Match the species

Cat, dog, bird, aquarium, and small-animal accessories are not interchangeable. Check the intended animal and ask a veterinarian when a care question goes beyond routine product selection.

Inspect regularly

Check toys, clips, cords, and habitats for wear. Replace damaged items and follow the manufacturer’s instructions.
